Evidence Base

Every claim graded by how provable it is. This is the reference the rest of the dojo is built on — search it, filter it, and notice how much of what circulates as "credit strategy" sits in tiers C, D, and F.

A — Statute / experimentBlack-letter law or binding regulation — or a randomised experiment or peer-reviewed meta-analysis. Does not vary by counterparty.
B — Policy / causalPublished official policy — or credible causal identification, or a replicated empirical generalisation. Real, but conditional.
C — PracticeWell-documented industry behavior. Not binding. Varies by who you are dealing with.
D — ContestedMechanism unverified, or a vendor benchmark, self-reported survey or unreplicated single study. A hypothesis to test, never a target.
F — False / illegalProvably wrong, the method is a federal crime, or no traceable primary source says what the claim says.

Tool Bench

Six working instruments. Change the inputs and watch where the hinges are. Every figure is sourced in the Evidence Base.

1 · S-Corp Election Simulator

Most calculators show payroll-tax savings and stop. This one also prices what the election costs you in QBI deduction — the term that flips the answer more often than people expect.

2 · Depreciation Engine — §179 & 100% Bonus

2026: §179 capped at $2,560,000, phasing out dollar-for-dollar above $4,090,000 (IRC 179(b); Rev. Proc. 2025-32). 100% bonus is permanent for property acquired after 19 Jan 2025 (IRC 168(k); OBBBA sec. 70301).

3 · QBI (§199A) Estimator

Made permanent by OBBBA sec. 70105. Phase-in widened to $75,000 / $150,000 for 2026, with a new $400 floor where QBI is at least $1,000.

4 · SBA Loan Cliff Finder

SBA 7(a) fees and rate caps step at exact dollar amounts. Loan sizing is normally treated as "how much do I need" — it should also be "which side of the cliff am I on." FY2026 fee schedule, SBA Information Notice 5000-872051.

FY2026 runs through 30 Sept 2026. The manufacturer fee waiver applies to loans of $950,000 or less and is set annually by SBA notice — it may not renew.

5 · Credit Ladder Diagnostic

Tiers cannot be skipped. Check only what is genuinely true today; the diagnostic finds your rung and hands you the next three moves.

6 · Funding Readiness Scorecard

What an underwriter sees when your file crosses the desk. Weighted, because not every line carries equal weight.

7 · Exit Tax Stack

The calculation almost nobody runs before buying. §1245 recapture is ordinary income with no cap; unrecaptured §1250 is capped at 25%. Character, not rate, is the part you control.

8 · Income Property Deal Screener

Cap rate, cash-on-cash, DSCR and the negative-leverage test in one place. The underwriting floors are pre-set to the ones that survive scrutiny.

Every tool here is a simplified teaching model. Real returns involve state tax, NIIT, AMT, basis, at-risk and passive activity rules, and facts these inputs never asked about. Use them to understand the shape of a decision, then take the decision itself to a professional.

Trials

Two games where the concept is load-bearing — you cannot win them by guessing, eliminating, or grinding. The Grading Trial makes you buy evidence before you judge. The Pitch Table makes you perceive quality before you produce it.

Before you start. These are meant to feel harder than reading. Effortful practice reliably teaches more than fluent explanation and reliably feels like it is teaching less — you are the worst-placed person to judge your own learning while you are doing it. If a trial feels uncomfortable, that is the mechanism working, not a defect.

試 The Grading Trial

A claim, stripped of its source. You have three evidence tokens. Spend them, then commit to a tier. Exact grade +3 · one tier off +1 · two off −2 · three off −3 · four off −4. Random play loses 0.36 points a round on average — the arithmetic is deliberate, so there is no way to farm this by guessing.

秤 The Calibration Trial

Sixteen claims, half of them widely believed and false. State the probability each is true and be scored by Brier. Being right is not enough — being right at the confidence you claimed is the whole test. This is the one skill in the dojo that cannot be transmitted by explanation; it moves only from scored feedback.

授 Teach-Back

A student arrives holding a specific wrong model and asks the follow-up that exposes whether you understand or are reciting. In every round the confident-sounding correction is the wrong one — the follow-up is written to catch it.

移 Transfer Trials

A problem you have not seen, in a domain the concept was not taught in. This is the only test that separates learning the idea from learning the examples — and it is deliberately uncomfortable.

眼 The Pitch Table

Two claims. One survives scrutiny; one does not. Pick the sound one and name the dimension that separates them — both must be right to score. Taste is calibrated judgment, and calibration comes from watching your prediction diverge from an expert's.

Drills

Recall beats recognition. Commit to an answer before you check — a wrong answer you thought hard about teaches more than a right one you guessed.

Arena

Case studies with consequences. Each has a defensible answer and at least one plausible trap. Choose before you read the debrief.

Red Flags

Claims that failed scrutiny, and arrangements the IRS has formally designated abusive. These are not grey areas — each is contradicted by a primary source. They appear here because they are actively marketed, often alongside legitimate planning.

Two tells worth memorising. First, any pitch citing a private letter ruling as authority — under IRC 6110(k)(3) a PLR may not be used or cited as precedent by anyone other than the taxpayer who requested it. Second, any arrangement sold on contingency as a percentage of tax saved, which aligns the promoter with the size of the deduction rather than its defensibility.

Participating in a listed transaction without filing Form 8886 carries a penalty up to $100,000 for an individual and $200,000 for an entity under IRC 6707A — whether or not the underlying position is ultimately sustained. IRC 7701(o) codifies the economic substance doctrine, with a 40% strict-liability penalty under IRC 6662(i) if undisclosed and no reasonable-cause defense.
