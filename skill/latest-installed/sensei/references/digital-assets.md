## Digital assets — the legal container

- **[A] The GENIUS Act is law.** *Public Law 119-27, enacted 18 July 2025, 139 Stat. 419* — the first federal framework for payment stablecoins: permitted issuers, reserve requirements, redemption rights, supervision.
- **[A] Its effective date is the EARLIER of 18 January 2027 or 120 days after the primary federal payment stablecoin regulators issue final implementing regulations** (GENIUS Act §20; restated verbatim by the Federal Reserve in its 24 September 2026 proposal). **18 January 2027 is a ceiling, not a floor** — late rules cannot push it out, they can only fail to pull it in. The agencies missed the statutory one-year rulemaking deadline of 18 July 2026, and a final rule issued after about 20 September 2026 cannot land 120 days before the backstop, so 18 January 2027 governs. **The deadline did not move. The compliance window compressed.** *Folded from the former sensei-live layer (now `live.md`) 2026-10-01: this line previously said "later of", which inverts the planning advice — anyone told "later of" plans to be ready after January 2027 and is non-compliant on day one.* Rulemaking status → `live.md` DA-02.
- **[A] Market structure is not settled.** The CLARITY Act **is not law**. Its legislative status — committee votes and the failed Senate cloture vote — is dated → `live.md` DA-01. Anyone describing SEC/CFTC jurisdiction as resolved is describing a bill.
- **[A] The DeFi front-end broker rule was repealed** by CRA joint resolution *H.J.Res. 25*, signed 10 April 2025 — and the CRA bars a substantially similar rule without new legislation. **Custodial broker reporting was not repealed.**
- **[A] A federal CBDC is prohibited through 31 December 2030.** The CBDC title of the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act bars the Federal Reserve Board and Reserve Banks from issuing one, carving out open, permissionless, private dollar-denominated stablecoins. Graded A on enactment. Enrolled text read 2026-10-01 (new Federal Reserve Act §16A, H.R. 6644, P.L. 119-101): the exception covers "any dollar-denominated currency that is open, permissionless, and private, and fully preserves the privacy protections of United States coins and physical currency"; "digital asset" takes the GENIUS Act definition; the section ceases to be effective on 31 December 2030; a rule of construction says nothing in it authorises a CBDC absent an Act of Congress. Vehicle and sunset traps → `live.md` DA-03.
- **[A] EO 14233 (6 March 2025) established a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve** from forfeited assets and directs that it not be sold. **[D] The commonly quoted holdings figure of roughly 328,372 BTC is an analytics estimate, not a Treasury balance-sheet disclosure.**
- **[B] Three states enacted "bitcoin reserve" laws in 2025 by three materially different mechanisms.** New Hampshire HB 302 *authorises* up to 5% of public funds into assets above $500B market cap. Arizona HB 2749 uses *unclaimed and seized* property. Texas SB 21 + HB 4488 created a reserve whose first purchase was **$5M of a spot ETF, not self-custodied coin**. All three were reported in identical language. **An authorisation is not a mandate, and buying an ETF is not holding the asset. Read the instrument** — the same discipline that separates a lease from a licence.
- **[A] SEC generic listing standards (approved 17 September 2025)** let qualifying spot commodity and crypto ETPs list without individual Section 19(b) filings, compressing timelines toward roughly 60–75 days. Actively managed, leveraged and novel-feature products still require 19b-4.
- **[C] The SEC "innovation exemption" and the five-category token taxonomy are agency positions and proposals, not codified law.** No final rule was locatable as of July 2026, and sources conflict on whether anything took effect in January 2026. Treat as proposed until a Federal Register final rule says otherwise. Newer SEC proposals → `live.md` DA-06.
- **[A] Nothing in this container carries deposit insurance.** FDIC insurance covers deposits at insured banks. SIPC does not reach crypto unless it is a security in a securities account. A stablecoin's backing is a private balance-sheet obligation — the GENIUS Act imposes reserve and redemption duties on issuers, **it does not make the government the guarantor.** The same asymmetry Sensei teaches everywhere else: *regulated means supervised, not safe.*

## Digital assets — tax, reporting and the balance sheet

- **[A] Digital assets are property, not securities, for federal tax.** Every disposition is a taxable event — including crypto-to-crypto swaps and spending it. **No like-kind treatment. §1031 was narrowed to real property in 2017 and does not reach digital assets** — a point the student who knows the real-property rules will assume wrongly.
- **[A] Because they are property, §1091 wash-sale does not apply.** Selling at a loss and repurchasing immediately is currently permitted. Repeal proposals have recurred annually since 2021 and **none has been enacted**; two vehicles were live in mid-2026, one drafted to reach tax years beginning after 2025. **Do not build a permanent plan on a loophole with an annual repeal attempt and a retroactive-effective-date draft in circulation.**
- **[A] Form 1099-DA: gross proceeds from 1 January 2025, cost basis from 1 January 2026** for covered assets acquired and held in the same broker account. Your records and broker-reported basis will diverge wherever assets moved between venues. Reconcile before filing, not after a notice.
- **[B] Global tax visibility arrives on a published schedule.** OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework: 48 countries collecting from 1 January 2026, first automatic exchanges **2027**, a second wave — Switzerland, Singapore, UAE, Hong Kong, Türkiye — in **2028**, and the **United States committed to first exchanges in 2029**. The EU implements through DAC8, applying from 1 January 2026 with first reports due between 1 January and 30 September 2027. Retail payment transactions above **USD 50,000** carry specific reporting rules.
- **[A] FASB ASU 2023-08 changed the balance sheet.** For fiscal years beginning after 15 December 2024, in-scope crypto is measured at **fair value with changes through earnings** — replacing the indefinite-lived-intangible model under which impairments were recognised but gains never were until sale. **Two consequences: pre-2025 and post-2025 financials are not comparable, and holdings now inject full two-way volatility into reported earnings.** A lender looking back three years is reading two different accounting regimes.
- **The second-order trap.** Sensei's standing rule — tax minimisation and borrowing capacity pull in opposite directions — has a new edge here. Crypto under fair value makes earnings swing both ways, and **volatile earnings are volatile earnings to an underwriter.** A treasury position taken for one reader is read by every reader, including the one pricing your next DSCR loan.
