## Business credit

- **[A] Business credit reports are largely outside the FCRA.** The dispute rights, 30-day clock, accuracy duties and private right of action that govern your personal file mostly do not exist for your business file. Errors there are a customer-service problem, not a statutory one.
- **[B] PAYDEX 80 = paying exactly on terms**, zero days beyond due. ~90 is about 20 days early; ~100 about 30 days early. Bands: 0–49 high risk, 50–79 moderate, 80–100 low risk. Paying on time *caps* you at 80.
- **[B] PAYDEX requires a D-U-N-S number and roughly three trade experiences** from two or more vendors before a score is calculated. The first objective is not a good score — it is having one.
- **[B] FICO SBSS blends business data with the owner's personal credit.** "Building business credit" never means the personal file stops mattering.
- **[A] SBA sunset the SBSS prescreen for 7(a) Small Loans.** *Procedural Notice 5000-875701.* Note: sources date this 16 Jan 2026 (SBA notice) versus 1 Mar 2026 (operational cutover) — flag the discrepancy rather than picking silently. Lender-level policy may still use the score.
- **[C] Inconsistent name, address and phone is the leading cause of fragmented business files.**
- **[C] Not all vendors report**, and those that do report to different bureaus. Verify before you buy.
- **[C] Most business cards pull personal credit and require a personal guarantee.**
- **[C] Entity type does not sever personal liability on a guaranteed obligation.**
- **[F] Shelf corporations do not carry transferable creditworthiness.**
- D-U-N-S numbers, EINs and SAM.gov registration are **free**. Paying for them is the tell of a low-value program — and for SAM.gov it matches a documented GSA/BBB fraud pattern.

## Structure and law

- **Piercing the veil** turns on totality: commingling (the heavyweight), undercapitalization, failure to observe formalities, alter ego, misleading third parties, and whether respecting the entity would sanction fraud. Sign as *[Name], Managing Member, [Company] LLC*.
- **Transferring assets after a claim arises is a fraudulent transfer** — unwindable, and it supplies evidence of intent.
- **Asset protection is layered:** insurance first, then separation (operating co / holding co / IP entity / equipment entity) — which only holds with written intercompany agreements at arm's length with payments actually made — then charging-order protection, which varies by state and is weaker for single-member LLCs.
- **QSBS (§1202) requires a C corporation.** Stock acquired after 4 July 2025: 50% exclusion at 3 years, 75% at 4, 100% at 5+; per-issuer cap **$15M**; gross asset ceiling **$75M**. Most professional services, financial services, hospitality and farming excluded.
