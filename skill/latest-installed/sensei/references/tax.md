## Tax doctrine

- **[A] Failure to file costs ten times failure to pay** — 5%/month versus 0.5%/month. An extension extends time to file, never time to pay. Not filing because you cannot pay is the most expensive common mistake in tax administration. *IRC 6651*
- **[A] The Cohan rule does not rescue travel, meals, gifts, or listed property.** IRC 274(d) expressly withholds estimation from exactly the categories people fail to document. *IRC 274(d), 6001*
- **[A] Assessment statute:** 3 years; 6 if 25% of gross income omitted; unlimited for fraud or non-filing. Basis records must be kept until 3 years after the return reporting the sale. *IRC 6501*
- **[A] Refund claims expire** 3 years from filing or 2 years from payment, whichever is later. *IRC 6511*
- **[A] Reliance on a professional is a real defence to the 20% accuracy penalty** — unless the adviser promoted the transaction or you withheld facts. *IRC 6662, 6664(c); Neonatology Associates, 115 T.C. 43*
- **[C] S-corp reasonable compensation is a facts test and low salaries lose.** *Watson, 668 F.3d 1008 (8th Cir. 2012); Radtke, 895 F.2d 1196 (7th Cir. 1990)*
- **[B] Home office requires regular AND exclusive use.** Safe harbour $5/sq ft to 300 sq ft. *IRC 280A(c)(1)*
- **[B] Without a written accountable plan, reimbursing yourself creates taxable wages** — and IRC 67(g) means you cannot deduct it personally either. *Reg. 1.62-2*
- **§179 vs bonus:** §179 is limited to business taxable income (excess carries forward); bonus has no income limit and can create a loss — which the excess business loss threshold may then suspend.
- **SSTB cliff:** health, law, accounting, actuarial science, performing arts, consulting, athletics, financial services, brokerage, and any business whose principal asset is the reputation or skill of its owners. Above the full phase-out the deduction is **eliminated entirely**, not reduced.
