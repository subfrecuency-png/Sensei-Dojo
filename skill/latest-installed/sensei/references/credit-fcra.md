## Credit reporting law (FCRA)

- **[A] §611 is the reinvestigation duty, not §609.** The bureau must complete reinvestigation within 30 days of notice; extendable to 45 only if the consumer supplies additional relevant information during the initial 30, and not extendable at all if the item is found inaccurate or unverifiable. Written results due within 5 business days of completion. *15 U.S.C. §1681i*
- **[A] §609 is disclosure only.** It entitles a consumer to the information in their file, its sources, and who received the report. It creates **no** duty to verify, no documentary production requirement, and **no basis for deletion**. The entire "609 letter" industry rests on citing the wrong section. *15 U.S.C. §1681g*
- **[A] Route disputes through the bureau, not the furnisher.** §623(a) imposes a general accuracy duty on furnishers but carries **no private right of action**. §623(b) does — and is triggered *only* when the furnisher receives notice from a consumer reporting agency. Disputing directly with the furnisher forfeits the enforceable claim. *15 U.S.C. §1681s-2*
- **[A] Method of verification is a 15-day right.** After reinvestigation the consumer may request a description of the procedure used, including the furnisher's name and address. A vague or non-responsive answer is itself evidence the reinvestigation was unreasonable. *§611(a)(7)*
- **[A] Reinsertion requires certification plus 5-day notice.** Deleted information may not be reinserted unless the furnisher certifies it is complete and accurate; the bureau must then notify in writing within 5 business days. Failure to send that notice is a discrete, provable violation. *§611(a)(5)(B)*
- **[A] Bureaus may refuse a dispute they deem frivolous**, with notice and reasons within 5 business days. This provision was written for mass-mailed template disputes and it works. *§611(a)(3)*
- **[A] Seven years — plus 180 days for charge-offs and collections.** The period runs 7 years from the expiration of 180 days after the date of first delinquency preceding the collection or charge-off. **DOFD is the single most important date on a derogatory tradeline.** *§605(a)(4), (c)(1)*
- **[A] Bankruptcy: 10 years** from the date of entry of the order for relief — the statutory maximum for all chapters. The 7-year figure commonly cited for Chapter 13 is voluntary bureau policy, not law. *§605(a)(1)*
- **[A] Identity theft block in 4 business days** on proof of identity, an identity theft report, identification of the information, and a statement it was unauthorized. A police report is not required — an FTC Identity Theft Report suffices. *§605B*
- **[A] Willful violations:** actual damages or statutory damages of $100–$1,000, plus punitives and fee-shifting. Negligent: actual damages plus fees. Fee-shifting is why FCRA cases are viable on contingency. *§616, §617*
- **[A] Security freezes are free nationwide**, placed within one business day online or by phone. A freeze does not affect your score. *§605A(i)*
- **[B] Tax liens and civil judgments were removed entirely** from consumer reports in 2017–2018 under the National Consumer Assistance Plan.
