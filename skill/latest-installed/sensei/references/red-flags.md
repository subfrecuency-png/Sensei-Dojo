## Red flags — say so plainly

**Two tells.** Any pitch citing a **private letter ruling** as authority (IRC 6110(k)(3) — a PLR binds only its requester). Any arrangement sold **on contingency as a percentage of tax saved**.

**IRS-designated abusive:** syndicated conservation easements (IRC 170(h)(7), Notice 2017-10) · abusive micro-captives under §831(b) (T.D. 10029; *Avrahami*, 149 T.C. 144) · monetized installment sales (REG-109348-22) · deferred sales trusts (no supporting authority) · the "self-employment tax credit" (does not exist) · overstated withholding (IRC 6702, 6663 — potentially criminal) · pure/constitutional trusts (*Lucas v. Earl*, 281 U.S. 111) · OIC mills · ghost preparers (IRC 6695(b)) · **any structure whose only purpose is tax** — IRC 7701(o) codifies economic substance, 40% strict-liability penalty under 6662(i) if undisclosed.

**Failed scrutiny — capital and structure:** "federal grants to start your business" · "SAT is $250,000" / "micro-purchase is $10,000" (stale since Oct 2025) · "you need SBSS 155/165" (stale) · "SBA guarantees 90%" · "SBA guarantees 50–85% on 7(a)" (that range imports SBA Express) · "an SBA loan is government money" (private loan, partial guarantee, personal guarantee standard) · "D-U-N-S/SAM.gov cost money" · "business purpose defeats CA usury" · "$50K–$250K business credit, no PG, no personal credit" (unsupported) · "microloans consolidate debt" / "504 for rental property" (prohibited uses; misrepresenting 504 occupancy is loan fraud) · "the 23% goal is set aside for my company" (agency-level goal; FY25 actual ~28%) · "8(a) is the fastest route" (fell to 3.7% / $24.3B in FY25) · **"overdraft fees are capped at $5"** (repealed by CRA, never took effect) · **"90% of startups fail"** (BLS: roughly half survive five years) · **"42% of startups fail from no market need"** (self-authored post-mortems of VC-backed companies only; categories overlap and do not sum to 100% — the publisher says so) · **"you need a warm introduction"** (the two instruments with the best causal evidence, SBIR and SBA, allocate by written proposal and by underwriting) · **"raise angel money for the Series A signal"** (the RD study found no effect on follow-on access) · **"you can pitch your raise publicly if the deck is good"** (506(b) prohibits general solicitation).

**Failed scrutiny — demand:** "the 8-second goldfish attention span" (traced to a defunct statistics site citing ~25 web visitors; the Microsoft report it is attributed to never says it) · "93% of communication is nonverbal" (Mehrabian studied single spoken words about feelings with conflicting cues, and has said so himself) · "the Rule of 7 touches" (no primary document; earlier practitioner estimates say ~20) · "$42 return per $1 on email" (marketers self-estimating, last-touch, published by email vendors) · "content marketing costs 62% less" (an infographic with no method) · "5x cheaper to retain than acquire" (untraceable; variant multiples from the same "source" are the tell) · "60/40 brand-to-activation is optimal" (award-entry archive with no failures, no control group, agency-authored, reverse causality unaddressed) · "10 points of ESOV buys 0.5–1 point of share" (same archive; growing brands mechanically buy more advertising) · "creative is 47% of ad effect" (variance decomposition where media barely varied — a range-restriction artifact) · "Rogers: 2.5% innovators" (areas under an assumed normal curve, not a measurement) · "Metcalfe's law, n²" (curve-fitted to two firms mid-growth) · "X% of influencer followers are fake" (every figure published by a company selling fraud detection) · "1,000 true fans" (a 2008 essay, not a study) · "engagement rate predicts sales" (correlations low to near-zero; engagement is selected on by ranking algorithms).

**Failed scrutiny — real property:** "bonus depreciation is phasing out, act now" (OBBBA restored 100% permanently for property acquired after 19 Jan 2025) · "1031 exchanges are going away" (OBBBA left 1031 unchanged; the $500,000 cap proposal failed) · "depreciation is free money" (a deferral with a character change — 1245 returns as ordinary income with no cap) · "cost segregation always pays for itself" (it fails when losses are trapped under 469, when 461(l) caps them, on short holds, on a rising bracket, or when a 163(j) election has you on ADS) · "the short-term rental loophole wipes out your W-2" (capped at $256,000 / $512,000 by 461(l), and hotel-like services add 15.3% self-employment tax) · **"put it in an LLC and you're protected"** (not protected by Garn-St Germain; a technical due-on-sale trigger; single-member LLCs get weaker charging-order protection) · "a real estate licence gets you REPS" (the test is hours and proportion of services, not licensure) · "an LLC or a living trust saves tax" (a disregarded entity and a revocable trust are both tax-neutral by design) · **"buy in a no-income-tax state, it's cheaper"** (Texas trades income tax for property tax levied on **gross asset value** whether or not the property earns — a worse risk profile for a leveraged owner; `[CA]` California's Prop 13 caps assessed growth at 2% a year, so the effective rate falls every year you hold) · **"the 1% rule tells you if a deal works"** (blind to taxes, insurance, vacancy, capex and management, and calibrated to a market that no longer exists — use it to reject, never to accept) · "the 70% ARV rule" (a margin heuristic that assumes your ARV and repair estimates are correct, which is exactly where flips fail) · **"triple net means you never touch it"** (NNN as marketed frequently leaves roof and structure with the landlord; only absolute net transfers everything — read the clause) · "wholesaling is legal everywhere because you're selling a contract" · **"the 2026 Cost vs. Value Report says…"** (no such edition exists; the current one is the 38th annual, September 2025) · "ADUs add 20–30% to value" (selection effect) · "real estate always goes up" (US residential fell nationally 2006–2012, many markets 30–50%; Class B office has repriced from about 6% caps to 8.5–11%, a value decline above 30% on unchanged income) · "cash flow is the only thing that matters" (year-one cash flow is the smallest component of long-run levered return behind amortisation, tax character and forced NOI — though negative cash flow remains a solvency risk that ends portfolios).

**Failed scrutiny — digital assets and markets:** "the CLARITY Act is law" / "market structure is settled" (a bill, not law — status → `live.md` DA-01) · "the SEC innovation exemption is in force" (no final rule locatable; sources conflict) · "the token taxonomy is settled classification" (agency position, not codified) · "stablecoins are FDIC-insured" (nothing in the container is) · "regulated exchange" offered as a safety claim (supervised ≠ solvent) · "proof of reserves proves solvency" (omits liabilities, point-in-time, cannot detect omitted accounts) · "audited" with no firm, no standard, no subject matter and no readable opinion · "Basel requires banks to hold X against crypto" (a standard binds nothing until a national regulator implements it) · "on-chain data proves entity X did Y" (the chain proves the transaction; attribution is inference with proprietary parameters) · any volume, liquidity ranking or volume-weighted index quoted without a wash-trading filter (peer-reviewed estimate: over 70% on unregulated venues) · "market cap" presented as money invested (it is last price × supply) · **any year-to-date figure with no as-of date** (the same year-to-date return circulated as two very different figures in one week, both true on different days — worked example `live.md` MK-01) · **any "average annual return" on a volatile asset** (quote the compound rate and the median, or say nothing) · "the four-year cycle says" (n = 3, no mechanism, promoted by holders, already diverging) · a single tokenized-RWA market-size figure (three aggregators spanned roughly $19bn to $29bn for the same quarter — that spread is definitional, not error) · a standard deviation with no stated estimator (sample and population differ by several percent under one label) · "past fund performance predicts future performance" (costs predict better) · **"90% of crypto tokens fail" quoted as though it described assets with real capitalisation** (the measured population is dominated by launchpad memecoins) · **"crypto is like-kind under 1031"** (§1031 was narrowed to real property in 2017).
